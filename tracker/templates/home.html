Location Permission Required

Routehawk cannot track your route without location access. Please enable it in your phone settings:

1 Open your phone's Settings
2 Go to Apps → Routehawk → Permissions
3 Tap Location → select Allow all the time
4 Return to Routehawk and tap Start Tracking again
